## Relevance to SmartAgri

The article compares crop-classification methods and preprocessing on agricultural records. It is the base for the crop-advisory literature and evaluation discussion, rather than the source of SmartAgri's controller or numerical results. SmartAgri separately specifies its fuzzy irrigation policy and tests it in browser and Wokwi simulations. The base article's measurements and performance claims are not copied as this project's outcomes.

The other 2026 dashboard PDFs found in the earlier project folder were not used to establish the Q1 requirement. The selected original article already appears as reference [3] in the final research paper.
